Privacy

What we keep

{{ site_name }} has no accounts. The only thing it keeps about you is the shows you save, in a cookie in your own browser (saved_shows). Clear it, or unsave the shows, to remove it.

Your location

If you give a ZIP code or town, or press "Use my location", the site shows how far each show is from there. Your location is kept in a cookie in your browser (near), rounded to about 100 metres, with the name you gave. It's sent to our server only so the page can show the distances, and the server doesn't store it. "Use my location" asks your browser for your position only when you press it. Follow "clear" next to the location to remove the cookie.

ZIP codes and towns are looked up in the U.S. Census Bureau's Gazetteer files.

Players from Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube

Some events have play buttons. Nothing loads from these services until you press one. Then the player is served by that service, under its own privacy policy.

{{ site_name }} uses YouTube API Services to find artists' own videos for its YouTube buttons. By using the YouTube player you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles what it collects under the Google Privacy Policy. The YouTube player comes from youtube-nocookie.com, which sets no cookies until you play a video.

Analytics

The site may count visits with an analytics service (GoatCounter or Google Analytics), which records the pages opened. Google Analytics' data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy linked above.
